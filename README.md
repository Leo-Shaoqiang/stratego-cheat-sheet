# Stratego Cheat Sheet & Battle Judge [![Web App](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Tool-StrategoCheatSheet.com-blue?style=flat&logo=safari)](https://strategocheatsheet.com/) [![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

An independent rules wiki, interactive combat calculator (Battle Judge), and tactical companion for the tabletop board game **Stratego**.

👉 **Access the live interactive tool**: [https://strategocheatsheet.com/](https://strategocheatsheet.com/)

---

## Features

- **Interactive Battle Judge (Combat Calculator)**: Settle combat resolution instantly without revealing your pieces' ranks to opposing players. Supports special interactions:
  - Spy defeats Marshal on attack.
  - Miner defuses and eliminates Bombs.
  - Equal ranks eliminate both pieces.
- **Classic vs Modern Rank Converter**: Seamlessly switch between Classic numbering (1 = Marshal, 9 = Scout) and Modern international numbering (10 = Marshal, 2 = Scout).
- **Comprehensive Piece Dossiers**: Ranks, movement capabilities, attack values, and strategic roles for all 40 pieces per army.
- **Official Rules & Reference**: Printable rule sheets, line of sight rules, two-square rule, more-squares rule, and lake obstacle navigation.
- **Winning Opening Setups**: 3 battle-tested 4x10 setup formations (Defense First / Flag in Corner, Blitzkrieg Attack, and Balanced Trap).

## Quick Reference: Rank Comparison

| Piece | Quantity | Modern Rank | Classic Rank | Special Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Marshal** | 1 | **10** | **1** | Highest rank; defeated only by Spy on attack or Bombs |
| **General** | 1 | **9** | **2** | Defeats 8 / 3 and below |
| **Colonel** | 2 | **8** | **3** | Mid-high officer |
| **Major** | 3 | **7** | **4** | Mid officer |
| **Captain** | 4 | **6** | **5** | Scout hunter / tactical flanker |
| **Lieutenant** | 4 | **5** | **6** | Recon support |
| **Sergeant** | 4 | **4** | **7** | Frontline infantry |
| **Miner** | 5 | **3** | **8** | **Only piece that can safely defuse and remove Bombs** |
| **Scout** | 8 | **2** | **9** | **Moves any distance in a straight line** (cannot jump) |
| **Spy** | 1 | **1** | **S** | **Defeats Marshal IF the Spy attacks first**; defeated by any piece when attacked |
| **Bomb** | 6 | **B** | **B** | Immobile; destroys any attacking piece except Miners |
| **Flag** | 1 | **F** | **F** | Immobile; capture to win the game immediately |
